PROCESS INFOGRAPHIC

/make-post — Visual Guide

Generates and launches a visual HTML index in the default browser mapping out all skills and when to use each.

01
/brand — Identity Onboarding

Conducts the 4-part interview (Niche, Vibe, Palette, Types) and establishes/refines brand.md.

02
/trend-scout — Market Discovery

Researches trending angles, viral hooks, and high-resonance themes into ideas.md.

03
/copy-hook — Copywriting

Crafts on-card micro-copy, scroll-stopping headline hooks, captions, and hashtag groups in caption.md.

04
/design-html — Code Graphic Artboard

Codes pure HTML/CSS graphics (1080px canvas, Google Fonts, CSS gradients, inline SVGs). Explicitly prohibits dashboards or web apps.

05
/render-post — Headless Compiler

Executes headless Chromium to compile index.html into high-resolution post.png with font preloading.

06
/carousel-split — Multi-Slide Decks

Generates structured sequential slides (slide-01.html, etc.) and batch-renders them into PNG sequences.

07
/critique — Taste & Review

Reviews post drafts with you, applies visual tweaks, and curates approved patterns and anti-patterns into likes.md.

08
/grid-preview — Feed Harmony

Simulates an Instagram 3x3 profile grid locally to inspect color rhythm and typographic cadence.

09
/publish-guide — Publishing Copilot

Formats copy-paste captions, image paths, alt-text, and guides upload on Instagram Web.
